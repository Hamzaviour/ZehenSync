How it works

One point of origin, three people who need it.

In Urdu, one nuqta turns د into ذ, the first letter of ذہن, mind. ZehenSync treats evidence the same way: every conclusion sits on a point you can find.

01 · For the person seeking support

One question. Fixed answers. Plain words.

The AI rewrites a validated questionnaire item so it reads naturally. The person taps an answer. The score is arithmetic in code, and the original wording is always one tap away.

  • PHQ-9, GAD-7, WHO-5 and the C-SSRS screener
  • Self-harm items are never reworded
  • A counselor chat that listens without diagnosing
02 · For the psychologist

Every flag has a source.

When a score jumps, the psychologist sees the rule that fired, the baseline, the exact answer and the words the patient actually saw, to the second. Then they decide.

  • Triage queue sorted by urgency and change
  • PDF and JSON exports with a content hash
  • Access only with the patient's consent, revocable any time
03 · For the psychology student

No source, no answer.

Answers are grounded in the DSM-5-TR and peer-reviewed papers, with the passage one click away. When the evidence isn't there, the tutor says so instead of guessing.

  • Adapts depth from first year to research level
  • Interview practice on synthetic patients
  • Never touches real patient data

What is required for a diagnosis of major depressive disorder?

Five or more symptoms must be present during the same two-week period, and at least one of them must be depressed mood or a loss of interest or pleasure.[1] The symptoms must cause clinically significant distress or impairment, and must not be better explained by a substance or another medical condition.[2]

For scale, a US national survey estimated lifetime prevalence at about 16%.[3]

Behavioural medical imaging

An ordinary webcam, read like a scan.

While a person answers, ZehenSync measures how they answer: blink rate, gaze stability, pauses in speech. Every measure carries a quality score, and dim or blurry windows are thrown out.

  • Blink rate and eye opennessEye aspect ratio from 478 face landmarks
    15 fps
  • Gaze stability and fixationsIris position, head pose, dispersion
    10 s windows
  • Speech rate and pausesFillers in English and Roman Urdu, pitch, jitter
    per clip

Computed inside the browser. Video never leaves the device, and markers sit beside the scores. They never raise a flag on their own.

Safety

Safety overrides everything.

Three detectors check every message and answer before any AI replies. When the signal is high, the AI goes silent and a person gets real help.

ModerateSafety pathway

Pause, ask gently, keep help in view.

The check-in pauses and the C-SSRS screener asks a few careful questions. Crisis numbers stay pinned for the rest of the session and the linked psychologist is alerted.

HighHard Stop

No AI text. A calm screen and a phone number.

Generation is aborted before it starts. The screen locks to crisis resources, and the psychologist is notified within a minute.

  • Never diagnoses, prescribes or predicts
  • Never argues with or confirms a delusion
  • Understands English and Roman Urdu
