In Urdu one nuqta turns د into ذ, the first letter of ذہن, mind. Calligraphers measure every letter in nuqtas. ZehenSync works the same way: every score, flag and citation sits on a point a psychologist can find and check.

Brand platform

Every conclusion has a point of origin.

For
People seeking support, the psychologists who review them, and the students learning the craft.
Promise
Screening you can trace, from flag back to the exact answer.
Character
Steady, exact, humane. A modern clinical journal with a Lahore accent.
Never
Alarm, diagnosis talk, wellness fluff, glowing AI.

03

Construction

Measured the way a calligrapher measures
Rules of the drawing
Axis
The mark mirrors itself across the 45° diagonal. The stem and the base are the same stroke, reflected.
Nib
Both terminals are cut at 24°, as if one broad pen drew them.
Stroke
11 units wide. The stem is four strokes tall, like a letter measured in nuqtas.
Nuqta
A 45° diamond, 13 units across, centred on the axis. It stays saffron in every colour version except single-colour print.
Clear space
Half a nuqta on every side of the mark; one full nuqta around lockups.
Minimum
Mark 16 px. Lockup 96 px wide. Below 16 px use the app icon.
Don'tRecolour the nuqta
Don'tMove the nuqta. It changes the letter
Don'tRotate the mark
Don'tOutline it
Don'tStretch it
Don'tAdd gradients or glow

06

Iconography

24 px grid · 1.75 stroke · square ends · nuqta detail

Custom icons share the mark's cut ends and carry a small nuqta where the idea has a point of focus. In an active state the nuqta turns saffron (last row, left). Everything else comes from Lucide at the same stroke weight. No brains, lightbulbs, robots or sparkles.

07

Pattern and image

Jaali lattice and single-ink halftone
Nuqta jaali · brand surfaces only
Image direction
Use
Single-ink halftone in neel. Lahore architecture, arcades, jaali screens, horizons, paper. The saffron sun is the only second colour.
Avoid
Stock photos of people holding their heads. AI-generated faces. Brains, neurons, glowing networks. Real patients, ever.
Where the pattern goes

Covers, decks, cards, social tiles, the login backdrop. Never behind patient questions, results or the crisis screen.

What is required for a diagnosis of major depressive disorder?

Five or more symptoms must be present during the same two-week period, and at least one of them must be depressed mood or a loss of interest or pleasure.[1] The symptoms must cause clinically significant distress or impairment, and must not be better explained by a substance or another medical condition.[2]

Before anything ships

Anti-slop checklist
  • No gradients, glassmorphism, glowing orbs, or neon.
  • No row of three feature cards with icons in coloured circles.
  • No emoji anywhere in the interface.
  • One saffron point per screen, at most.
  • Every number is real or labelled demo data.
  • Every image explains something only ZehenSync does.
  • Newsreader, Schibsted Grotesk and Geist Mono. Nothing else.
  • No stock photos of distress. No AI faces. No brains.
  • The mark appears as drawn: no effects, no rotation.
  • Copy passes the voice table.
