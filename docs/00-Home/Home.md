# ZehenSync AI

Screening support for mental health, built for three people with three different jobs:

- **Patient**: takes guided screenings, talks to a CBT-style counselor, sees their own history in plain language.
- **Psychologist**: reviews a triage queue, traces every AI flag back to the exact input that caused it, exports clinical data.
- **Psychology student**: studies with a cited tutor that refuses to guess, and practices interviews on synthetic patients.

The product screens. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or treat.
